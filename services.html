Accredited Assurance Pathway

Two-Step ISO Certification Process

Our structured appraisal and partner-verification methodology delivers internationally recognized certification with zero operational bottlenecks.

1
ON-SITE PHASE

Expert Facility Appraisal

Following completion of press standardization and process baseline preparations, our lead engineering team conducts a thorough on-site physical appraisal of your operating machinery, energy flows, and quality monitoring systems.

  • Physical sampling and equipment calibration reviews
  • Production proofing and run-sheet conformity testing
  • Gap-analysis and remediation report within 48 hours
2
VERIFICATION & ISSUANCE

Independent Partner Assessment

The second-stage assessment is executed in conjunction with our authorized ISO Certification partner through rigorous examination of verified proof sheets, OK sheets, and print production manifests.

Official ISO 12647-2 Certification
Upon meeting all criteria, your formal accredited certificate—valid for 24 months—is officially issued by the certification body and delivered directly to your enterprise.
